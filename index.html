01 / About

We are BSIT 1A.

Four first-year students, one program, one portfolio.

We are taking the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. This website documents our first year: our class activities, quizzes, laboratory work and exams.

Each member has an academic archive for our grading periods, so everyone's own work is kept in one place, and a resume that can be viewed and downloaded in the Resume Library.
